Wisdom & mythology

Manvori's retrieval layer is designed to connect questions with a managed Indian-wisdom corpus rather than relying only on generic generation.

How we build

Tradition with a product mindset.

The current Manvori architecture combines a Flutter mobile client with a Go API, MongoDB persistence, Redis-compatible infrastructure, asynchronous workers and a multi-provider AI layer.

Grounded retrieval

Traditional sources are retrieved from a managed corpus and passed as context. The system is designed not to invent source citations.

Scalable conversations

Messages live separately from conversation headers and use keyset pagination, keeping long-running threads practical.

Behaviour insights

Explicit product visit events support product analysis while avoiding chat text in analytics metadata by default.

Trust & transparency

Clear about what Manvori is.

The public site and legal pages are designed to be useful for users, app reviewers and cloud-program verification.
