Inside Manvori

Designed around how people actually explore.

Every screen below is a photograph of the app running, not a mockup. Manvori is Telugu first: the interface, the daily reading and the answers are all in Telugu, and anything worth hearing can be read aloud.

01 / 06

Today's context

The day opens with the panchanga — tithi, nakshatra, abhijit muhurtam and the rahu, yamaganda and gulika windows — with a listen control, because this is read every morning by people who would rather be told than read.

02 / 06

Ask Manvori

The primary action is a conversation. Answers come back in Telugu, grounded in the person's own chart, and every one of them can be listened to rather than read.

03 / 06

South Indian Kundali

The birth chart in the South Indian square layout — houses one to twelve, planets named in Telugu, the lagna and nakshatra in the centre, and the doshas called out plainly.

04 / 06

The details people are asked for

రాశి and నక్షత్రం lead, because those are the two things a person is actually asked for. Behind them sit the lagna, the nakshatra lord, the running maha and antar dasha, and the gana, nadi and yoni a match is judged on.

05 / 06

Soul Room

A separate, quieter conversation surface for personal reflection. What is said here stays outside the normal conversation history.

06 / 06

Everything in one place

Six ways in — jyotishyam, numerology, panchanga, the Soul Room, the people in your life, and your own record of decisions. Simple first, deeper only when it is wanted.

How we build

Tradition with a product mindset.

The current Manvori architecture combines a Flutter mobile client with a Go API, MongoDB persistence, Redis-compatible infrastructure, asynchronous workers and a multi-provider AI layer.

Grounded retrieval

Traditional sources are retrieved from a managed corpus and passed as context. The system is designed not to invent source citations.

Scalable conversations

Messages live separately from conversation headers and use keyset pagination, keeping long-running threads practical.

Behaviour insights

Explicit product visit events support product analysis while avoiding chat text in analytics metadata by default.

Trust & transparency

Clear about what Manvori is.

The public site and legal pages are designed to be useful for users, app reviewers and cloud-program verification.
